# Ranger Rumble Offline

**English** · [Français](README.fr.md)

A tool to keep playing *Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble* after its servers close on May 1, 2027.
For Windows and macOS, in 6 languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.

## iPhone and Android

- **The save copy is made on iPhone or iPad only.**
- **The offline game (the mod) is for Android only.**
- **A copy made on iPhone works on Android**: your iPhone progress goes on in the offline game, on
  an Android phone or tablet.

## What the tool does

The tool opens on a home screen with the game's pictures. Choose where your game is (iPhone or
iPad, or Android), then follow the steps.

1. **Copy my save** (iPhone and iPad only, before May 1, 2027): the iPhone scans a QR code and
   installs a profile. When the game starts, the tool keeps a copy of your save and of the game's
   data. Nothing to set by hand. On Android, there is nothing to copy: you start a new game.
2. **Install the game** (Android only): the tool builds a version of the game that works without a
   server (Android APK), from the official 1.10.4 game you provide, with your iPhone save or a new
   game. Then it installs it on the phone by cable.
3. **Edit a save**: heroes, levels, weapons, costumes, medals, currencies, statistics... From a
   file, a copy or straight from the phone by cable.
4. **Updates**: updates the tool, then the phone's game, without losing your progress. The
   offline game announces new versions itself, in its inbox.

## New features and changes from the original game

The whole game works without a server: shop, missions, events, Ranger Road, friends.

**Heroes**

- The 7 heroes that were never released: Molly, Helen, Terry, Crumby, Chance, Flare and Nakari.
- Juycee and Rivet on top. Rivet has Kit on her back, her own hammer and a jump attack.
- The added heroes are earned on the Ranger Road; Nakari is bought in the shop. Each of them has
  an ultimate attack.
- Costumes that were only downloaded online (Molly, Terry).

**Weapons**

- Each hero has their main weapon, and a gadget of your choice as secondary weapon.
- A hero's weapon and gadget unlock with them; weapons without a hero are bought in the shop.
- A weapon that was never released: the Scorpion Flail.
- The home screen shows the hero's weapons again, as in older versions.
- The drill's damage is shown.

**Modes and events**

- Bolt Rush together, and Bolt Rush alone with its King, as in version 1.1.0 (difficulty in the
  game's settings).
- A 16-player Battle Royale.
- The Mrs. Zurkon and random weapon events, as the developers had planned them, with the
  collection screen from version 1.1.0.

**Playing together**

- Friends on the same Wi-Fi show up in the game: invitations and squads of 3.
- Remotely, with the game's squad code.
- Squad battles work in every mode except Mob Rush.

**Shop and rewards**

- Plan offers in Qredits, Qredits exchanged for Raritanium.
- No more offers for a hero or a weapon already at max; when everything is at max, random draws
  give Qredits.
- Raritanium in the daily rewards (days 3 and 7).

**Everything else**

- An artbook in the game's menu: game pictures, concept art, map layouts.
- A gallery for your own pictures (Social media > ARTWORK).
- The game in Polish, on top of its languages.
- The game announces the tool's new versions in its inbox.

## Install

Download `OutilSauvegardeRR.zip` from the latest version (**Releases** tab), then unzip it.

- **Windows**: the first time, double-click `Lancer (Windows).bat`. It installs what is needed,
  then creates the "Ranger Rumble hors ligne" shortcut on the Desktop.
- **Mac**: open "Ranger Rumble hors ligne". If the Mac refuses: System Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Open Anyway.

The first installation takes a few minutes (Python is downloaded if missing). Everything is
explained step by step in `LIS_MOI.txt`.

You also need the official 1.10.4 game (`.xapk`, `.apkm` or a complete `.apk`), 5 GB free, and a
64-bit Android phone or tablet (Android 7.1 or newer).

## What the tool contains, and what it does not

The tool's code, our changes to the game and the offline server, with the artbook (pictures of
the game and its promotion), costumes and parts of older versions (the 7 unreleased heroes from
1.10.3, the collection screen from 1.1.0), and card pictures made for the project. **Not the game
itself**: each player provides their own official copy (1.10.4), and the tool changes it on their
computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, apart from the question "is there a new version?" asked to
GitHub.
