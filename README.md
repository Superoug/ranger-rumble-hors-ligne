# Ranger Rumble Offline

**English** · [Français](README.fr.md)

Keep playing *Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble* after its servers close on May 1, 2027.
Windows and macOS, in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.


**Installing Python 3.15 beforehand is recommended.**


## iPhone and Android

- **The save copy works on iPhone or iPad only.**
- **The offline game runs on Android only.**
- **An iPhone copy carries on in the Android game.**

## What the tool does

1. **Copy the save** (iPhone and iPad, before May 1, 2027): scan a QR code, install a profile, open
   the game. The tool keeps your save and the game's data. On Android there is nothing to copy:
   the game starts from a new game.
2. **Install the game** (Android): builds the offline game from your official 1.10.4 game, with
   your iPhone save or a new game, then installs it on the phone by cable.
3. **Edit a save**: heroes, levels, weapons, skins, medals, currencies, stats. From a file, a copy
   or straight on the phone.
4. **Update**: the tool and the phone's game, by cable, without losing progress. The offline game
   announces new versions in its Inbox.

Deleting the game also deletes its save.

## What's new compared to the original game

The whole game works offline: shop, missions, events, Ranger Road, friends.

**Heroes**

- 7 new heroes: Molly, Helen, Terry, Crumby, Chance, Flare and Nakari.
- Rivet (a mod, not a character planned by the developers).
- Nakari costs 750 Raritanium.
- The new heroes are on the Ranger Road.
- Orbs for each new hero, in the shop or in Reebos. They stop once the hero is at max level.

**Weapons**

- Two kinds: main weapons and gadgets.
- Every weapon and gadget can be equipped on every hero.
- Weapons and gadgets unlock on the Ranger Road.
- New weapon: the Scorpion Flail.
- The home screen shows the hero's weapons again.
- Weapons and gadgets go up to level 10 again.

**Modes and events**

- Bolt Rush, co-op and solo.
- 16-player Battle Royale: turn on "Prototype maps and modes" in the game's settings.
- The Mrs. Zurkon and random weapon events, as the developers planned them.

**Playing together**

- Friends on the same Wi-Fi show up in the friends list: invitations and squads of 3.
- Remote play with a squad code, up to 3 players.
- Squad battles in every mode except Mob Rush.

**Shop and rewards**

- Plan offers in Qredits, Qredits exchanged for Raritanium.
- More Raritanium in the daily rewards (days 3 and 7).

**Other**

- An artbook in the game's menu: game pictures, concept art, map layouts.
- Four difficulty levels: easy, normal, hard, ultra hard.
- A new language: Polish.

## Install

1. Download `Launcher_Ratchet_Ranger_Rumble.zip` from the latest version (**Releases** tab) and
   unzip it.
2. **Windows**: the first time, double-click `Lancer (Windows).bat`. It installs what is needed and
   adds the "Launcher Ratchet Ranger Rumble" shortcut to the Desktop.
   **Mac**: open "Launcher Ratchet Ranger Rumble". If the Mac refuses: System Settings > Privacy &
   Security > Open Anyway.
3. The first start takes a few minutes (Python is downloaded if missing). Step-by-step help:
   `Read me.txt`.

**Installing Python 3.15 beforehand is recommended.**

You also need the official 1.10.4 game (`.xapk`, `.apkm` or a complete `.apk`), 5 GB free, and a
64-bit Android phone or tablet (Android 7.1 or newer).
